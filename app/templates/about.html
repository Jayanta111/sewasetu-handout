About the pension

The Old Age Pension Scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Purvanchal, under the State Social Assistance Programme.

Who can apply? Residents of Purvanchal who are at least 60 years old on the day they apply. Please submit one application per person.

The department should decide an application within 15 days. Under the Right to Public Services Act, an application left undecided after that period is deemed approved.

Scheme guidelines: OAP Guidelines 2023 (PDF)
